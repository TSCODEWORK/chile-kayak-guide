About this guide

Chile Whitewater Kayak Guide is a field atlas and a sibling of Chile Water Levels. Live numbers appear only when that site’s gauge is the run itself. Readings are provisional. Scout before you put on.

Pucón Kayak Retreat

Pucón Kayak Retreat (PKR) is a credited partner, based on the Río Trancura in Pucón. Araucanía is their home water, and a natural base for the runs around Villarrica.

Live water

Where a run has a public station, the page links to ChileWaterLevels, which reads Dirección General de Aguas telemetry.

On the river

Many of these valleys are Mapuche and Pehuenche territory. Ask before you cross a gate, pay local access where it’s customary, and leave nothing behind.
